Privacy Policy

Patakha — Diwali Crackers Simulator · ARTIN Studios · Effective 3 October 2026

Summary

ARTIN Studios runs no servers and does not itself collect personal data. There are no accounts. The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells optional one-time upgrades through Google Play. Those Google services process some data, as described below. The website version has no ads and no purchases.

Microphone

Only if you tap the diya button, the app listens to your microphone to tell when you blow on the phone, so it can put out the diyas. The sound is analysed live on your device to measure its loudness and pitch. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and listening stops when you tap the button again or leave the app.

Flashlight and camera

Only if you switch on flashlight bursts. The Android app controls the flash LED directly and never captures camera images. The website version has to ask your browser for camera access to reach the flashlight. It does not look at, record or send any image.

Greeting card

The greeting card is drawn on your device from the fireworks on your screen. If you type a name, it is only drawn onto the image. It is not stored or sent anywhere. The card goes only where you choose to share or save it, using your phone's own share sheet.

Motion sensor

Only if you switch on "Shake phone to light", the app reads your phone's motion sensor to notice a shake. The readings are used instantly on your device and never saved or sent.

Vibration

Used to make your phone vibrate with each cracker. Nothing is saved or sent.

Advertising (Android app)

The free version shows a banner and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to unlock a cracker for 24 hours. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction and diagnostic data, to serve, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying Remove ads or the Festival Pack turns banner ads off.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which upgrades your Google account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, the number of crackers you have lit, and any 24-hour unlocks are kept in local storage on your device so the app remembers them. They never leave your device and are deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Internet

The simulator is bundled inside the Android app and works offline. Internet access is used only to load ads and to talk to Google Play about purchases. The website loads its own files from its host and nothing else.

Children

Patakha is not directed at children under 13. Ads are requested as general-audience, non-child-directed traffic.

Shubh Deepavali · शुभ दीपावली

All the dhamaka.
None of the smoke.

Anar, chakri, rockets, ladi and sutli bombs with sound made for each cracker. Your phone vibrates with every blast and the flashlight flashes with it.

  • Turn the volume up, ideally with headphones or a speaker.
  • Vibration follows each cracker's sound.
  • Blow on the mic to put out the diyas, then tap them to light them again.

A simulator for fun. No real fire, no smoke, no noise for your neighbours' pets.

A green and safe Diwali

If you do burst real crackers tonight:

  • Prefer green crackers, and follow your city's rules on timings.
  • Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby and wear cotton clothes.
  • Light at arm's length, then walk away. Never relight a dud.
  • Keep children supervised, and think of pets, older people and patients nearby.
